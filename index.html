Privacy Policy

For game version 1.1 and later · Effective date: 11 November 2026

Arrow Out: Logic Puzzle is provided by Stillwave Studio. This policy explains what information the game uses and who receives it.

Information saved on your device

The game saves completed levels, stars, unlocked levels, your current attempt, hint and undo counters, coins, daily reward progress, arrow colour themes you own, whether you bought "Remove ads", tutorial status and sound preferences. These records stay in the app's local storage so you can continue playing. The developer does not receive them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: short ads between some levels, and optional video ads you can choose to watch for a reward such as a hint or extra coins. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the game's Settings ("Ad privacy choices"). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. Google processes the payment; the developer does not receive your payment details. The game only records on your device that the purchase was made, and Google Play can restore it when you reinstall the game with the same Google account.

Ratings

The game may show Google Play's in-app review window. Your rating and review are handled by Google Play under Google's policies; the game does not see them.

What the developer collects

Stillwave Studio does not operate a server for this game, has no accounts, and does not collect, sell or share your personal information. Information used for ads and purchases is handled by Google as described above.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. Ads are limited to content rated for teens.

Storage, retention and deletion

Your game records remain in the app's local storage until you clear that storage or uninstall the game. To delete information held by Google for ads, use your Google account and Android advertising settings.
