Privacy Policy

For game version 1.0 · Effective date: 17 October 2026

Arrow Out: Logic Puzzle is provided by Stillwave Studio. This policy explains the information used by this version of the game.

Information saved on your device

The game saves completed levels, stars, unlocked levels, your current attempt, hint and undo counters, tutorial status and sound preferences. These records let you continue playing and retain your settings.

Information sent to the developer

The game has no account, advertising, purchases, developer analytics or developer-operated online service. The Android release does not request Internet, camera, microphone, contacts, location or Advertising ID permissions. The developer does not receive your gameplay records through the game and does not sell or share them.

Storage, retention and deletion

Your game records remain in the app's local storage until you clear that storage or uninstall the game. Local records use Android's app storage protections. The developer does not hold a server copy of your gameplay.

Android backup or device-transfer features, if enabled, depend on your device and account settings. The game does not provide its own cloud-sync service. The developer cannot recover progress that is no longer available on your device or through its backup facilities.

Support and privacy questions

For support or privacy questions, contact Stillwave Studio at sufianwp97dot@gmail.com.

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to answer your request and resolve support issues. Messages are handled through our email provider, Google Gmail. Please avoid sending passwords or other sensitive information.

We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to handle your request and meet applicable legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your support correspondence by emailing the same address. We do not sell support information or use it for advertising.

This policy website

This page contains no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies added by Stillwave Studio. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in GitHub's hosting documentation. This website-hosting activity is separate from the offline game.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date if the game's data practices change.
